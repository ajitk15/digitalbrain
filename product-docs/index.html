How these pages are written

  • From the code. Every statement was checked against the source on main. Module names appear in code style so a technical reader can go straight to it; a non-technical reader can skip them.
  • "Why" boxes - Why - say what a choice protects and, where the codebase records it, what was tried or rejected instead.
  • Motion in diagrams shows flows. The Motion button at the top pauses it, and it stays still if your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Light and dark follow your system; the sun button switches them.
