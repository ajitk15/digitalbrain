# Digital Brain graph generation — four-minute student video

The MP4 explains three fictional health insurance documents becoming a graph. It has nine chapters, 1080p visuals, English captions and local Indian-English narration using Microsoft Heera (en-IN). The diagrams illustrate the real application's pipeline; this is not a live UI recording.

The visual focus is the difference between structural document nodes and AI-proposed business relationships, followed by quote/type checks, merging, optional embeddings, Graphify grouping and human publication. On-screen technology labels identify the actual packages and responsibilities.

The project builder, validator, entity merging and Graphify were executed on normalized synthetic sources with prepared extraction proposals. Actual output: nine structural nodes and six structural edges; enriched graph: thirteen total nodes and fifteen edges, including four business entities and three business relationships. An invented quote was rejected.

Upload conversion, paid provider extraction/embedding calls, saving and publication are illustrated, not performed in this example. Source words uses and explains are displayed in the diagram; the saved ontology normalizes them to depends on and covers. Embeddings and AI enrichment are optional. Scanning and OCR depend on deployment settings.

To reproduce: run prepare.py, generate local narration with narrate.ps1 through Windows PowerShell, then run build_video.py and verify_video.py with project Python. Requires Windows en-IN Heera voice, Pillow, FFmpeg and FFprobe. build_video.py reads ../insurance-revalidation.json, generated by ../revalidate_insurance.py. Audio timing is normalized modestly to fit exactly four minutes; per-chapter measurements are in speech-metrics.json. Caption timing is estimated by phrase length, not word-aligned transcription.

Files: graph-generation-4-minutes.mp4, transcript.md, captions.srt, narration.wav, storyboard.json and verification.json. The editable Python and PowerShell files remain beside the outputs.
