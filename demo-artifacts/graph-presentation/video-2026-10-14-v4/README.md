# Layered circular-node graph generation video

Four-minute, 1920 x 1080 MP4 with Microsoft Heera Indian-English synthesized narration, burned-in captions, selectable captions and nine navigation chapters.

One fixed left-to-right canvas replaces changing slides. Icons identify source documents, conversion, structural nodes, AI proposals, validation, databases, vectors and publication. Focus borders follow the narration. Three insurance statements illustrate subject, relationship and object. The normal structural graph appears first, revealing the document node, heading and sentence nodes in sequence. AI enrichment then reveals entity circles, the first relationship, and relationships from the other two documents. Completed layers remain visible. Evidence checks, embeddings, Graphify and publication appear as their explanation begins.

## Evidence and scope

The example uses the project graph builder, prepared AI proposals, evidence judge, entity merging and Graphify grouping validated in ../insurance-revalidation.json. Upload/conversion, external AI requests, embedding calls, persistence and publication are illustrations, not a recorded live application run. The documents are fictional teaching examples. Graphify groups existing nodes; embeddings support later retrieval rather than prove graph relationships.
