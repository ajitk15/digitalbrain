Remove only the white/off-white background and make it genuinely transparent with an alpha channel, including white areas inside all letter counters and symbol cutouts. Keep every foreground element unchanged and fully intact: identical indigo db monogram geometry, exact Digital Brain lettering, colors, proportions, spacing, placement and canvas composition. Do not redesign, recolor, retype, move or crop any foreground element. Preserve clean smooth antialiased edges without white halos. Output transparent PNG, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no replacement background, no shadows.
