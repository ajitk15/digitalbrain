How triage works

When an incident comes in, triage looks through your own records for anything that explains it, asks the AI for up to three ideas based only on what it found, checks every quote, and tells you how strong the evidence is. It suggests; you decide.

The six steps

  1. Profile symptomsPicks out the words that describe the fault, ignoring ids, dates and filler, and recognises common phrasings of the same fault — "504", "gateway timeout" and "timed out" all count as a timeout.No AI
  2. Gather evidenceFinds similar past incidents, changes on the same component in the 48 hours before, other open incidents like this one, and runbook passages. Words that are rare in your incidents count for more than common ones, so "FHIR" matters more than "export".No AI
  3. Ask the AIOnly if there is evidence. Personal details are removed first. The AI may only use what step 2 found.AI{% if model_label and model_label != 'AI off' %} · {{ model_label }}{% endif %}
  4. Verify citationsEvery quote must appear word for word in a live record, and every suggested check must be read-only. Anything else is dropped.No AI
  5. Rate the evidenceScores the evidence from 0 to 1 using fixed rules — the AI does not grade itself.No AI
  6. SaveKeeps the run, numbered, so you can come back to it and compare it with later runs.No AI

Reading the result

Evidence strength
Too weak (under {{ insufficient_below }}): no ideas are shown — investigate by hand. Low (under {{ low_below }}) and Medium: ideas worth checking. There is no High yet: that needs a record of which ideas turned out right.
Ideas to check
Up to three explanations, each with the records it quotes. They are leads, not a diagnosis.
What to check first
A safe, read-only step to confirm or rule out the idea. Nothing is ever changed for you.
Was it useful?
Your answer is the outcome record that will let the strength rating be checked and calibrated. Please answer once you know.

Cost and safety

  • About 30 seconds per run and one AI call{% if typical_cost %}, typically {{ typical_cost }} here{% endif %}. Running it again on unchanged evidence reuses the earlier answer and costs nothing.
  • Triage only reads. It never changes ServiceNow, Jira or any system.
  • If a record it quoted changes later, the old result hides itself and asks you to triage again.

Service
The business service the component belongs to.
Similar past incident
A resolved incident on the same component, service or symptoms, with how it was fixed.
Recent change
A change request on the same component that started shortly before the incident.
